MODERN WARFARE CHESS: RULES & GUIDE (HINGLISH)

🎯 Khel Ka Maksad (How to Win):

Har khiladi ke paas 32 Modern Military Units hain. Jeetne ka sabse seedha tarika hai dushman ke 🏛️ Command Bunker (HQ) ko tabah karna! Agar aap dushman ke dono 💰 Gold Vaults (GLD) tod dete hain, toh dushman ki heavy machines (Tank, Jet, Bomber, Submarine) ka fuel khatam ho jata hai aur wo sirf 1 kadam chal paati hain!

🟢 Green Box = Normal Move

Khali zameen ya samundar mein apni unit ko aage badhane ke liye Green box par click karein.

🔴 Red Box = Melee Capture

Purane chess ki tarah dushman ke khane mein ja kar usey tabah karne ke liye Red box dabayein.

🟠 🎯 Orange Box = Ranged Strike!

Naya Rule: Artillery (🎯), Submarine (🚤) aur Air Defense (🚀) bina apni jagah se hile door se missile maar kar dushman ko uda sakte hain!

🗺️ 3 Tarah Ke Ilake (16x16 Battlefield Terrains):

  • 🌊 Sea Lanes (Kinare ke 2-2 Columns): Yahan sirf Submarine (🚤), Commando (🥷) aur udne wale Air Units (✈️, 🚁, 🛸) ruk sakte hain. Tank ya Sainik paani mein nahi ja sakte.
  • 🪖 Land Grid (Beech ke 12 Columns): Yahan Tank, Artillery, Sainik, Gold Vault aur HQ chalte hain.
  • ⛏️ Center Gold Mines (Beech ke 4 Sone ke Khane): Apni units ko yahan rakhne par aapka Economic Control badhta hai.
