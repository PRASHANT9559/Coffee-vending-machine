# IMPORTANT RESEARCH RULES

1. Sirf obvious AI Agents ki list mat banao.
2. Real-world business workflows ko decompose karo.
3. Ek broad “Marketing Agent” likh kar khatam mat karo.
4. Marketing ke andar actual tasks aur specialized agents identify karo.
5. Same principle har industry par apply karo.
6. Duplicate agents ko merge karo.
7. Lekin genuinely different workflows ko alag agents rakho.
8. Current AI capabilities aur near-future agent capabilities ko alag identify karo.
9. Speculative science-fiction agents ko include mat karo.
10. Practical computer-use agents ko priority do.
11. APIs ke bina browser/desktop automation possible hai ya nahi, separately identify karo.
12. Human approval wale workflows ko clearly mark karo.
13. High-risk domains me human oversight requirements clearly mention karo.
